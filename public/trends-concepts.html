Four ways to make the fleet's production sing

Same real data contract (monthly_by_year, seasonal_yoy, fleet totals), four "go-crazy" presentations, all on the Array Operator brand. Live mock fleet: 7 arrays, 3 years of seasonal solar production.

A Liquid Energy
A living, flowing gradient fill for the current year, energy as fluid. Prior years are ghost ridgelines behind it. The surface gently undulates like sun on water; the leading edge has a bright meniscus glow.
B Solar Spiral
The climate-spiral lineage. Twelve months wrap a clock; each year is a glowing ring whose radius is that month's fleet kWh. Seasonality becomes a shape you can read in one glance, the summer bulge, and growth pushes each year's ring outward.
C Energy Ridgeline
A joyplot. One luminous ridge per year, stacked back-to-front, summer peaks become a mountain range. Year-over-year growth reads as each ridge rising a little higher than the one behind it. Scales gracefully to a decade of history.
D Production Heat-Field
The higher-dimensional read: a month × year grid where every cell glows with its production. Patterns jump out instantly, bright summer bands, the diagonal of fleet growth, any dark month an array went down. Hover-ready, dense, and calm.
