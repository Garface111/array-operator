Privacy Policy — Array Operator


**Last updated:** July 14, 2026

**Effective for:** Array Operator mobile app (Capacitor / iOS / Android), mobile web at arrayoperator.com/m/, and related owner-facing product surfaces.


**Company:** Array Operator / EnergyAgent

**Contact:** support@arrayoperator.com · admin@nepooloperator.com


This is a **draft** policy for store submission and product transparency. Have counsel review before relying on it as a final legal document.


---


1. Who we are


Array Operator (also associated with EnergyAgent / NEPOOL-related operator tooling) provides software for solar and energy asset owners and operators: fleet monitoring overview, offtaker invoicing, vendor connections, and an in-product **Energy Agent** assistant.


2. Scope


This policy describes how we handle information when you:


  • Create or use an **Array Operator account**
  • Use the **mobile app** or **mobile web** client
  • View **fleet telemetry** and related operational data
  • Manage **offtaker** records and **invoices**
  • Use **Energy Agent** (chat / assisted actions)
  • Maintain a signed-in **session** on your device

It does not cover third-party sites or monitoring vendor portals you connect on your own (SolarEdge, inverter portals, payment processors, etc.), except as those services send data to us under your authorization.


3. Information we collect


3.1 Account and profile


  • Name, email address, and authentication credentials (or magic-link tokens)
  • Organization / owner profile fields you provide
  • Billing and plan-related information needed to operate the subscription
  • Support messages you send to us

3.2 Fleet telemetry and operational data


  • Array, site, inverter, and related identifiers you configure
  • Production, status, and other monitoring metrics ingested from connected vendors or tools you enable
  • Connection metadata (e.g. which vendor is linked, last harvest / refresh times)
  • Configuration you set for monitoring and capture modes

3.3 Offtaker and invoice data


  • Offtaker names, contacts, commercial terms, and allocation details you enter
  • Invoice content, PDF previews, send status, and payment-related flags (e.g. Stripe Connect enablement)
  • Records needed to generate, deliver, and troubleshoot invoices

3.4 Energy Agent


  • Messages and prompts you send to the Agent
  • Context the product attaches so the Agent can answer (e.g. selected offtaker or fleet summary)
  • Pending actions and your **confirmations** before write operations
  • Technical logs needed to improve reliability and safety of Agent features

3.5 Sessions, device, and usage


  • Session tokens and authentication cookies (e.g. `so_session`)
  • Approximate device/app version, OS, and diagnostic logs
  • IP address and basic request metadata for security and abuse prevention
  • Product analytics limited to operating and improving the service (not for selling profiles)

3.6 What we do **not** do


  • We **do not sell** your personal information or fleet/offtaker data.
  • We **do not** use your telemetry or invoices to build advertising profiles for third parties.
  • We **do not** require access to your phone contacts, photos, or microphone for core fleet/invoice features (unless a future feature asks with a clear purpose and permission).

4. How we use information


We use the data above to:


  • Authenticate you and keep sessions secure
  • Display fleet status and operational insights
  • Create and manage offtaker invoices and related workflows
  • Run Energy Agent and execute only the actions you confirm
  • Connect to monitoring and payment providers **you** authorize
  • Provide support, fix bugs, and improve reliability
  • Meet legal, accounting, and security obligations

5. Legal bases (where applicable)


Depending on your jurisdiction, we rely on one or more of: performance of a contract (providing the service you signed up for), legitimate interests (security, product improvement, fraud prevention), consent (where required, including certain integrations), and legal obligation.


6. Sharing


We share information only as needed to run the product:


| Recipient | Purpose |

|-----------|---------|

| Infrastructure / hosting providers | Host app, API, databases, files |

| Monitoring vendors you connect | Exchange credentials/tokens and telemetry under your connection |

| Payment processors (e.g. Stripe) | Billing and offtaker payment features you enable |

| Email / messaging providers | Auth magic links, invoice delivery, service notices |

| Professional advisors / authorities | When required by law or to protect rights and safety |


We do **not** sell personal data. We do not share fleet or offtaker data with unrelated marketers.


Service providers are expected to process data under agreements appropriate to their role (including confidentiality and, where required, data-processing terms).


7. Retention


  • **Account data** — retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, disputes, and legal retention.
  • **Telemetry** — retained according to product configuration and operational needs; older aggregates may be kept longer than raw high-frequency points.
  • **Invoices / offtakers** — retained for the life of the account and typical business/tax record periods unless you request deletion where law allows.
  • **Agent conversations** — retained to provide history and improve safety; you may request deletion subject to technical and legal limits.
  • **Sessions** — expire per product policy; you can sign out to end a device session.

8. Security


We use industry-standard measures appropriate to a multi-tenant SaaS product (encrypted transport, access controls, session authentication). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong unique password and protect magic-link emails.


9. Your choices and rights


Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or to object to / restrict certain processing. To exercise rights or ask questions:


  • **Email:** support@arrayoperator.com
  • **Alt:** admin@nepooloperator.com

You may also close your account through product account flows where available, or by contacting support.


You can sign out of the app to clear the local session. Uninstalling the app removes the client from your device but does not automatically delete server-side account data.


10. Children


Array Operator is a business / professional tool. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.


11. International transfers


We may process data in the United States and other countries where we or our providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.


12. Third-party services


If you connect SolarEdge, inverter manufacturer portals, Stripe, or other third parties, their privacy policies apply to data those services hold directly. Review those policies before connecting accounts.


13. Changes


We may update this policy as the product evolves. We will revise the “Last updated” date and, for material changes, provide additional notice (e.g. email or in-app) when appropriate.
