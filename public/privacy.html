Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026 · EnergyAgent (Array Operator · NEPOOL Operator · Solar Operator)

  • We never sell your data. It is used only to run your EnergyAgent reporting and monitoring service.
  • We only read your own utility and solar-inverter data — nothing else from your browser.
  • We never receive your portal passwords on our servers. Optional auto-login credentials are encrypted and stored only on your device.
  • You can delete everything by emailing admin@solaroperator.org — purged within 24 hours.

This Privacy Policy explains what EnergyAgent (a product of Dyson Swarm Technologies) collects, why, and how we protect it, across the Array Operator, NEPOOL Operator, and Solar Operator web applications and the EnergyAgent browser extension (the "Service"). It works together with our Terms of Service.

What we collect

Account information. Your name and email (your email is your login), your plan and billing status, and basic usage/diagnostic logs needed to run and secure the Service.

Data from the portals you connect. When you sign in to one of your own supported portals and connect it, the extension reads — using the browser session you already hold for that site — and sends to your EnergyAgent account:

  • From your utility portal (Green Mountain Power, or any NISC SmartHub utility): your utility account name, username/email, the meters on your login (account number, nickname, current-bill link), and your billing / generation history.
  • From your solar inverter portal (SolarEdge, Fronius/Solar.web, SMA/Sunny Portal/ennexOS, or Chint/CPS): your site list and per-inverter production data (serial, model, current power, energy today, daily history) — the same numbers shown on the portal's own dashboard.

Information you enter. Details you add to operate your account — for example offtaker/customer names and contact details and array configuration — so the Service can prepare reports and invoices for you.

Optional auto-login credentials. If you choose the optional "sign in once" feature, your portal username and password are encrypted and stored only on your own device (in the browser extension's local storage) and are used solely to keep that portal's session fresh. They are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

How we use it

  • To provide the Service: consolidate your data, monitor performance, and prepare your reports and invoices.
  • To operate billing, send service and account emails, and provide support.
  • To secure the Service, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

How we share it

We share data only with service providers ("subprocessors") that help us run the Service, under contract and only as needed:

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of our users or the Service. We do not otherwise share your data with third parties.

Security

  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Auto-login credentials are encrypted and kept on your device, not on our servers; stored vendor connection secrets are encrypted at rest.
  • After a capture, the extension clears the portal session cookies it used.
  • Access to production data is restricted and logged.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to fix issues promptly.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. You can disconnect any account at any time, and you can request deletion of your data by emailing admin@solaroperator.org; we purge it within 24 hours of a verified request, except limited records we must keep to comply with law (e.g., billing records).

Your choices and rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the CCPA or GDPR; we honor verified requests to exercise them and will not discriminate against you for doing so.

Children

The Service is for businesses and adults; it is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notified by email.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: admin@solaroperator.org.
